# Hopla — icon v2

## Concept
The hop-h: a single unbroken stroke rises as the stem of a lowercase "h" and then leaps over the arch, but instead of landing it lifts off. A gap one stroke wide is the hop, and the mint node below is the peer that catches the message. The small sky-blue dot, floating apart from the letter, is the optional Internet gateway: there when needed, never required.

## Rationale
- Silhouette: an "h" plus two dots. No other messenger has this shape (none of Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal, Bridgefy, Briar or FireChat use a letter, bubble, plane or radio arcs in this way).
- It still reads at 29 px: the 132-unit master stroke is about 3.7 px at 29 px, and the gap stays open.
- One logic throughout: the stem and arch share one stroke with round caps, the node radius is 0.62 of the stroke, the gateway radius is 0.38 of the stroke, everything sits on a 64-unit grid (1024 canvas), and the arch circle has r = 168 (see construction.png).
- Depth: a gentle diagonal gradient from bright to core to deep, plus a 14% radial light at the top left. It is matte, with no gloss and no bevel.

## Do / Don't
- Do: keep the hop gap open, keep the gateway dot detached, use glyph.svg on light backgrounds, and use monochrome.svg/icon-tinted.svg on system themes.
- Don't: join the arch to the node, recolour the node red or orange, add text, outlines, shadows or gloss, rotate the mark, or place it on a busy photo without its tile.

## Improvements on v1
v1 (three nodes and two arcs) read as a generic network diagram. v2 ties the meaning (hop, peer, gateway) to the name's initial, so it has an ownable silhouette and stays readable at small sizes. It is also built on a documented grid and has full platform variants (dark, tinted, Android adaptive and monochrome).

Files are generated by gen.py (`python3 gen.py`, needs rsvg-convert). The wordmark uses custom monoline geometric letterforms drawn as stroked paths; no font is involved.

## Wordmark (v2.1)
- Five letters drawn as one geometric monoline family and built by `wordmark.py` + `wm_build.py`. Strokes are converted to filled outlines (shapely buffer and union), so there are no live strokes or fonts and it renders the same everywhere.
- Metrics, in wordmark units (stroke 64, round caps, the same logic as the mark's 132 stroke): ascender edge 40, x-height edge 150, baseline 450, descender edge 572. The h and l ascenders are identical. The p descender (122) is close to the ascender (110), so it balances.
- Round letters (o, p bowl, a bowl, h shoulder) overshoot by 2 units (h shoulder 8) for optical alignment. The o, p and a share one bowl radius (126).
- Spacing: sidebearings are tuned per pair. Round to straight is 40–44 and the l tail to the a is 30, so o-p, p-l and l-a read evenly.
- The l ends in a short rightward tail: a quiet nod to the hop's lift-off that still reads clearly as an l.
- Files: wordmark.svg (ink #0A1F1E), wordmark-dark.svg (paper #F4F7F5), lockup(-dark).svg, readme-header-light/dark.svg (640×160, transparent).

- Don't: stretch it, track it out, swap in a font, or set it below 16 px height (use the glyph instead).
